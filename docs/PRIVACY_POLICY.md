# Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2026-11-11

HalalCheck (we, us, our) operates the HalalCheck mobile application (the App). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.
## 1. Information We Collect

- Account data: Email, password, and profile data when you sign up or sign in (email/password or Google Sign-In).
- Location data: Your geographic location when you enable location permission (venue map, suggest a venue).
- Camera / images: Photos you take of ingredient labels or packaging, processed on-device for halal analysis.
- User-generated content: Venue submissions and reviews you post.
- Device and usage data: Device model, OS, app version, and usage statistics.


## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the App and its features (scanner, map, venue suggestions, reviews).
- Verify your identity and manage your account.
- Improve the App and personalize your experience.
- Send you important account and security notices.

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. You must be 13 years of age or older to create an account. All sign-up pages include an age-verification step requiring you to confirm you are 13 or older.

## 4. Third-Party Disclosure

The App sends certain information to third-party services outside our control:

- Firebase / Google: User authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and hosting.
- Google Sign-In: Authentication via Google accounts (Google may process this data).
- Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org): Public product database queried for barcode lookups (product name, brand, ingredient text).
- OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org): Map tiles for the venue map.
- Google Maps (google.com/maps): Directions services when you tap "Get Directions".
- Cached network images: Product packaging images retrieved from third-party sources.

Your personal data, location data, and user-generated content may be transmitted to and processed by these third parties, which may be located outside your country.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App and comply with our legal obligations. You may delete your account and associated data at any time from the App's settings.

## 6. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

## 7. Data Subject Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us or by deleting your account within the App.

## 8. Marketing Communications (CAN-SPAM)

We may send you marketing emails from time to time. If you subscribe to our newsletter:

- Every marketing email includes a clear and conspicuous unsubscribe link.
- Every marketing email includes our physical postal address.
- Every marketing email identifies a clear sender name and sender email address.
- We honor opt-outs promptly (within 10 business days).
- You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email.
