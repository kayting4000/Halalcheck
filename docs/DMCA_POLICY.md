# DMCA Takedown Policy

HalalCheck ("we", "us", "our") is an online service provider and respects the intellectual property rights of others. This policy describes how to report alleged copyright infringement and how we respond.

## 1. What Is a Valid DMCA Takedown Notice?

To submit a notice, provide:

1. A physical or electronic **signature**.
2. Identification of the **copyrighted work** you claim has been infringed.
3. Identification of the **content** that is claimed to be infringing (e.g., a venue name, review text, or URL).
4. Your **contact information** (mailing address, phone number, and email address).
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a **good-faith belief** that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner.
6. Your **name** (or an agent's name) and a statement that the information is **accurate** and that you are **authorized** to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

## 2. How to Submit

Email your notice to: **[your-dmca-agent@example.com]**.

## 3. Our Response

We will respond to your notice promptly (within a few business days). If the notice is valid, we will remove or disable access to the content and, where appropriate, forward the notice to the content submitter.

## 4. Repeat Infringers

We reserve the right to terminate the accounts of users who are repeat infringers of intellectual property rights, in appropriate circumstances.

## 5. DMCA Agent Registration (Required)

To comply with the U.S. DMCA, online service providers must **register a designated DMCA agent** with the U.S. Copyright Office at **http://copyright.gov** (select "Online Service Provider" and complete the "Registrations for Notification of Claimed Infringement" form).
